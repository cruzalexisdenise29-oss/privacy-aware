KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

DATA PRIVACY ACT OF 2012

Republic Act No. 10173

What is RA 10173?

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 is a Philippine law that protects the fundamental human right to privacy of communication while ensuring the free flow of information for innovation and growth.

It regulates how personal information is collected, stored, processed, used, and shared by organizations and individuals covered by the law.

Why is it important?

  • Protects personal information.
  • Promotes responsible data handling.
  • Helps prevent unauthorized access.
  • Encourages transparency and accountability.
  • Provides rights to data subjects.

Your Data Privacy Rights

  • Right to be informed.
  • Right to access.
  • Right to object.
  • Right to erasure or blocking, subject to legal conditions.
  • Right to damages.
  • Right to file a complaint.
  • Right to data portability.
